# Learnings

## Depth-2: Broad Research

### L1: Graphic Design Research Methodology
- Graphic design analysis operates through **Analysis** (deconstruction of existing work) and **Synthesis** (creation of new solutions from analytical insights)
- Matt Cooke's 4-stage transferable methodology: **Definition → Divergence → Transformation → Convergence**
- Field of study research: understanding competing visual messages, audience context, visual saturation
- Primary research (direct testing, surveys) vs Secondary research (existing analysis) vs Tertiary research (summaries)
- Semiotics, communication theory, semantics, rhetoric, discourse theory are core analytical frameworks
- The "reflective practitioner" model: designers documenting their processes and intentions

### L2: POD Design Trends 2025-2026
- Apparel holds **39.45% revenue share**, remains #1 POD category
- Home decor is **fastest-growing** at 24.2% CAGR
- **82% of consumers** want personalized products
- **7 top-selling t-shirt design trends:**

  2. Art-only designs (pure visual/aesthetic expression)
  3. Statement and cause designs (identity, beliefs, symbols)
  4. Animal-inspired graphics
  5. Y2K and '90s nostalgia
  6. Sustainability-themed
  7. Hand-drawn and sketch-style
- DTG printing suits detailed/colorful designs; embroidery for simple/premium
- Mixed-media designs (photos blended with illustrations) trending

### L3: T-Shirt Design Mistakes & Best Practices
- **Common mistakes:** Poor font choice, too many colors, unflattering contrast, plain photos, wonky placements, overused clipart, inaccurate mockups
- **Best practices:** Understand target audience, match decoration technique to design, follow print file requirements (300 DPI for DTG), make trends work for you, experiment with samples
- Design placement dimensions: center chest 3-4" below neckline, left chest 7-9" from shoulder seam, full front ~11x13" adults
- Keep designs ~70% of shirt width for balance
- Horizontally center both visually AND mathematically (heavy elements can feel off-balance)

### L4: Design Element Analysis Frameworks
- **Color Theory:** Color wheel relationships (complementary, analogous, triadic, tetradic, split-complementary, monochromatic)
- **60-30-10 rule:** 60% dominant color, 30% supporting, 10% accent
- **HSL model** more intuitive for analysis than RGB (Hue 0-360°, Saturation 0-100%, Lightness 0-100%)
- Extraction tools: Adobe Color, Coolors, Colormind — use K-Means Clustering algorithms
- **Typography framework:** Font classification → Hierarchy analysis → Spacing (kerning/tracking/leading) → Visual weight assessment
- Leading should be ≥20% greater than font size
- **Composition framework:** Grid system identification → Rule of Thirds application → Golden Ratio (φ ≈ 1.618) → Visual hierarchy → Focal point analysis
- Visual hierarchy established via: Size, Color/Contrast, Placement, Whitespace, Typography, Texture, Repetition, Movement/Direction

## Depth-1: Focused Research

### L5: AI-Powered Design Analysis (Gemini Vision)
- Gemini can analyze: lighting setup, camera focal length, color palette (with hex codes), art style keywords
- Prompt template for analysis: `[Subject] + [Action] + [Location/context] + [Composition] + [Style]`
- Style transfer workflow: `[Reference images] + [Relationship instruction] + [New scenario]`
- Best practices: Be descriptive, iterate and refine, use reference points, specify what to keep, handle multimodal inputs coherently
- Example analysis prompt: "Analyze this image as if you are a professional photographer and 3D render artist. Break down the lighting setup, camera focal length, color palette (with hex codes), and specific art style keywords."

### L6: POD Design Anatomy — Layers & Structure
- **Layer structure:** Background elements → Main graphics/illustrations → Typography overlay
- Design illustration process: Research → Sketching → Inking → Coloring → File preparation
- Font selection criteria: brand alignment, legibility (especially from distance), mood-appropriate
- Font pairing rule: max 1-2 complementary font families
- Placement categories: Center chest, Left chest, Full front, Upper back, Full back, Sleeves
- Creative placements: lower corner, collar-hugging, sleeve hem, side seam split, diagonal chest

### L7: Visual Research Methodology (Academic)
- Noble & Bestley's framework from "Visual Research: An Introduction to Research Methodologies in Graphic Design"
- Chapter structure: Why and How → Methods: Ways of Thinking → Visual Research → Audience and Message → Process and Materials → Synthesis
- Design as problem-solving: practical problems (cost, production, technology) vs research problems (conceptual, theoretical)
- The designer's "field of operation" extends beyond commercial facilitation to authorship
- Recoupment: mainstream culture's ability to accommodate outsider/avant-garde ideas
- Epistemology in design: understanding the body of accepted knowledge (legibility, language, typography) plus borrowed theories (gestalt, rhetoric)
